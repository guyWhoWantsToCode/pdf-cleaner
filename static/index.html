    Review your PDFs, one at a time

    Choose a folder. PDF Cleaner finds every PDF in it and shows them one by one, so you can decide what to keep.

    • Private. Runs only on this computer. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.
    • Safe. Delete always asks first and moves the file to the Recycle Bin. Nothing is permanently deleted.
    • Untouched. PDFs are only read, never changed. Folders and other files are never touched.
